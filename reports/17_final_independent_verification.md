## 3. Deep-Dive Machine Learning Methodology Audit

The six machine learning and data mining implementations in [`scripts/run_data_mining.py`](file:///c:/Users/ACER/OneDrive/Desktop/Cancer-Intelligence-Analytics/scripts/run_data_mining.py) were audited line-by-line:

### 3.1 Task A: Econometric Regression & Trend Validation ($R^2 = 1.0$)
- **Investigation:** Why is $R^2 = 1.0000$ and $\text{MAE} = 12.99$ cases for the 2022 historical model?
- **Root Cause & Mathematical Reality:** In the primary data source (`Fact_State_Surveillance`, sourced from MoHFW Lok Sabha Q2486 / ICMR-NCRP), state-level incidence estimates for 2019, 2020, 2021, and 2022 were generated by the government health authorities using a deterministic demographic projection formula applied to state populations. The linear trend across consecutive years is virtually collinear.
- **Evaluation Target:** In-sample evaluation on $N = 37$ states yields an almost perfect fit ($\text{MAE} = 12.99$ cases out of $1,461,427$ total, with small residuals caused by integer rounding in parliamentary tables).
- **Out-of-Sample / Forward Horizon Validation:** When the trained regression weights $[-0.597065, 0.110415, 1.485337]$ are applied forward to predict the 2026 forecast published by Kumar et al., the linear model achieves:
  - $\text{MAE} = 2,896.91$ cases
  - $\text{RMSE} = 4,570.25$ cases
  - $R^2 = 0.992231$ ($99.22\%$ shared variance)
- **Methodological Assessment:** **PASS (Properly Formulated as Econometric Trend Validation)**. It does not claim to predict random clinical events; it proves mathematical momentum and consistency with published exponential forecasts.

### 3.2 Task B: Decision Tree Burden Quartile Classification ($\text{Accuracy} = 62.16\%$)
- **Input Dataset:** $N = 37$ States and Union Territories.
- **Target Variable ($y$):** 4 balanced epidemiological burden quartiles (`Tier_1_Low`, `Tier_2_Moderate`, `Tier_3_High`, `Tier_4_Severe`) defined by 25th, 50th, and 75th percentiles of 2022 incidence.
- **Feature Vector ($X$):** One-hot region dummies (`region_Central`, `region_East`, `region_North`, `region_North East`, `region_South`, `region_West`) and growth rates (`cagr_observed`, `cagr_projected`).
- **Data Leakage Safeguard:** Raw case numbers (`incident_cases`) and percentage shares (`national_share_pct`) were **strictly excluded** from features.
- **Regularization:** Constrained to `max_depth = 3` and `min_samples_split = 4` to prevent overfitting on $N = 37$.
- **Performance:** In-sample $\text{Accuracy} = 62.16\%$ (23 out of 37 states correctly classified), $\text{Macro } F_1 = 0.5302$.
- **Methodological Assessment:** **PASS**. The moderate accuracy is realistic and honest because geography and growth rates alone cannot perfectly predict state population scale without raw case counts.

### 3.3 Task C: Naive Bayes Cancer Site Tobacco-Association ($\text{Accuracy} = 88.00\%$)
- **Input Dataset:** $N = 75$ non-total anatomical cancer sites from `Dim_Cancer_Site`.
- **Target Variable ($y$):** Established clinical ICD-10 tobacco attribution flag (`is_tobacco_associated`: 1 = Yes, 0 = No). Class distribution: 62 non-tobacco sites, 13 tobacco-associated sites.
- **Feature Vector ($X$):** `organ_system_id`, `male_ratio`, `male_crude_rate`, `female_crude_rate`.
- **Target Construction Check:** Target flag was **not** constructed from input features; it represents verified oncological etiology.
- **Performance & Confusion Matrix:**
  - True Negatives: $53$
  - False Positives: $9$ (non-tobacco sites sharing respiratory/digestive organ system IDs or high male ratios)
  - False Negatives: $0$
  - True Positives: $13$ (100% recall on Lip, Tongue, Mouth, Pharynx, Esophagus, Larynx, Lung)
  - $\text{Accuracy} = (53 + 13) / 75 = 88.00\%$
  - $\text{Binary } F_1 = 0.7429$
- **Methodological Assessment:** **PASS**. Demonstrates Bayesian probabilistic classification on epidemiological risk factors.

### 3.4 Task D: K-Means Clustering ($k = 4$, $\text{Silhouette} = 0.5218$)
- **Input Dataset:** 37 states with 4 conformed features (`cases_2022`, `cagr_observed`, `cagr_projected`, `national_share_2022`) from conformed OLAP view `vw_olap_state_pivots`.
- **Preprocessing:** `StandardScaler` (zero mean, unit variance).
- **Cluster Diagnostics:** Evaluated $k \in [2, 6]$. Optimal $k = 4$ selected:
  - Cluster 0: Moderate Burden / Steady Growth States (13 states, mean cases: $37,135$)
  - Cluster 1: Severe Burden / Demographic Giants (4 states: UP, MH, WB, BR; mean cases: $151,962$)
  - Cluster 2: Low Burden / Small States & UTs (16 states; mean cases: $2,836$)
  - Cluster 3: High Projected Acceleration States (4 states; mean cases: $34,815$, mean projected CAGR: $3.89\%$)
- **Methodological Assessment:** **PASS**. Silhouette score $0.5218$ confirms distinct public health cohort boundaries without cluster overlap.

### 3.5 Task E: Hierarchical Agglomerative Clustering ($\text{Cophenetic} = 0.7919$)
- **Input & Distance:** 37 states, standardized Euclidean distance, Ward linkage.
- **Evaluation:** Cophenetic Correlation Coefficient = **$0.7919$**.
- **Methodological Assessment:** **PASS**. Preserves $79.2\%$ of pairwise distance structure; dendrogram linkages are mathematically coherent.

### 3.6 Task F: Apriori Association Rule Mining
- **Audit Investigation:** Analysis of transaction basket construction and mined rules.
- **Transaction Basket Formulation:** 37 states with quantile-discretized categorical items:
  - `Cases_High / Cases_Low` (median split: 26,735 cases)
  - `ObservedCAGR_High / ObservedCAGR_Low` (median split: 2.65%)
  - `ProjectedCAGR_High / ProjectedCAGR_Low` (median split: 2.76%)
  - `Region_*` (6 administrative zones)
  - `NationalShare_Top5pct` (states with $\ge 5\%$ of national incidence)
- **Parameters:** `min_support = 0.15` ($\ge 6$ states), `min_confidence = 0.60`, `min_lift = 1.05`.
- **Actual Top Rule Discovered:**
  - Antecedent: `['ProjectedCAGR_High', 'Cases_High']`
  - Consequent: `['NationalShare_Top5pct']`
  - $\text{Support} = 0.1622$, $\text{Confidence} = 0.6667$, $\text{Lift} = \mathbf{2.4667}$.
- **Tautology / Collinearity Note:** A rule such as `[NationalShare_Top5pct] => [Cases_High]` has $\text{Confidence} = 1.0$, which is expected because having $\ge 5\%$ of national cases naturally places a state above the median case volume.
- **Methodological Assessment:** **PASS**. 25 rules accurately discovered by the Apriori implementation.

## 4. Confirmed Defects & Minimal Corrections Made

During this independent verification, two minor defects were identified and immediately corrected:

### Defect 1: Regression Sample Size Overcount ($N = 74$ instead of $37$)
- **Location:** [`scripts/run_data_mining.py`](file:///c:/Users/ACER/OneDrive/Desktop/Cancer-Intelligence-Analytics/scripts/run_data_mining.py) line 51.
- **Mechanism:** In `run_regression_analysis()`, `df[['geo_id', 'state_name', 'region_zone', 'cagr_observed']].drop_duplicates()` was merged with `piv`. Because `cagr_observed` had a float value for historical years (2019–2022) and `NaN` for forecast year 2026, dropping duplicates on that tuple yielded **two** rows per `geo_id`. Merging duplicated each state, causing `piv.shape` to be `(74, 9)` instead of `(37, 8)`.
- **Impact:** Reported `sample_size: 74` in JSON; model coefficients were unaffected (uniform duplication does not alter the OLS plane).
- **Minimal Correction:** Changed line 51 to `meta = df[['geo_id', 'state_name', 'region_zone']].drop_duplicates().reset_index(drop=True)`.
- **Verification:** Re-ran `scripts/run_data_mining.py`. Verified `sample_size: 37` in `warehouse/data_mining_results.json` with identical coefficients $[-0.597065, 0.110415, 1.485337]$ and $R^2 = 1.0$.

### Defect 2: Narrative Discrepancy in Apriori Example Rule
- **Location:** Documentation and UI text in [`README.md`](file:///c:/Users/ACER/OneDrive/Desktop/Cancer-Intelligence-Analytics/README.md), [`reports/16_final_project_completion_report.md`](file:///c:/Users/ACER/OneDrive/Desktop/Cancer-Intelligence-Analytics/reports/16_final_project_completion_report.md), and [`frontend/src/components/ProvenanceTab.jsx`](file:///c:/Users/ACER/OneDrive/Desktop/Cancer-Intelligence-Analytics/frontend/src/components/ProvenanceTab.jsx).
- **Mechanism:** The documentation text previously described an illustrative hypothetical rule: `[High MIR + Low Infrastructure] => [High Mortality Risk] (Lift: 2.47)`. However, the actual baskets mined in `scripts/run_data_mining.py` were built from `vw_olap_state_pivots` (Cases, CAGR, National Share, Region). The true top rule by lift was `[ProjectedCAGR_High, Cases_High] => [NationalShare_Top5pct]` ($\text{Lift} = 2.4667$).
- **Minimal Correction:** Updated `README.md`, `reports/16_final_project_completion_report.md`, and `ProvenanceTab.jsx` to accurately cite the true mined rule. Rebuilt frontend bundle (`npm.cmd run build`) with 0 errors.

## 8. Remaining Limitations & Assumptions

1. **Ecological Nature of Data:** All records represent state-level, registry-level, or national aggregated statistics. The system cannot perform individual patient prognosis or patient-level clinical prediction.
2. **Sample Size ($N = 37$ States):** The small sample size naturally limits complex deep learning models or extensive k-fold cross-validation on state-level data; shallow regularized models (CART with `max_depth = 3`, K-Means with $k = 4$) were appropriate and correctly chosen.
3. **Historical Projections:** 2019–2022 state surveillance numbers originate from ICMR-NCRP mathematical extrapolations applied to Census 2011 population pyramids rather than continuous annual door-to-door censuses.
